Auditing interpretation
----------------------
The default overall estimate is 55.09% at the pooled variant level and 58.94% at the distinct-product any-variant level.
These values answer different questions and should be reported as complementary prevalence estimates, not as competing estimates of one identical population parameter.
Portal-specific estimates show the result within each retailer–market source. They do not establish representativeness beyond the four observed portals.
